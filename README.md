## 📋 Features

### Resume analysis
Upload your resume in PDF or Word format. The system scans your document against specific job descriptions. It identifies keywords you might miss and highlights areas where your skills do not match the job requirements.

### Skill gap detection
The software looks at the skills listed in your resume versus the skills requested in a job posting. It provides a list of specific skills you should add or emphasize to improve your chances of getting an interview.

### Resume optimization
Get suggestions to rewrite your professional summary and experience bullet points. The tool uses language patterns that help your resume pass through automated filters. 

### Cover letter generation
Create professional cover letters tailored to each job application. You provide the job description, and the software generates a draft that highlights your relevant experiences.

### ATS scoring
Applicant Tracking Systems often reject resumes that do not meet specific formatting or content criteria. HireMind-AI gives you a score based on your resume structure and keyword density. Use this score to track your progress as you edit your documents.

## 💡 How to use the tool
